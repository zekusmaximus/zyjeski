THE VERDANT ACCORD explores the ethics of technological control versus organic adaptation, asking what cities become when we build them to grow with people rather than despite them. It's a hopeful interrogation of who gets included in "acceptable" futures and what we lose when we optimize away the messy, beautiful chaos of human resilience.

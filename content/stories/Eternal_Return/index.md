In the beginning, there was nothing. And then, there was everything.

The Eternal Return is not merely a story—it's an exploration of consciousness, existence, and the infinite cycles that bind us all. What begins as a simple question about identity spirals into a profound examination of reality itself.

Through nine chapters and an epilogue, we follow the protagonist's journey through different realms of existence, each revealing deeper truths about the nature of self, time, and the eternal recurrence of all things.

This tale weaves together philosophy, mysticism, and speculative fiction to ask the fundamental questions: Who are we? Why do we return? And what does it mean to truly awaken?
